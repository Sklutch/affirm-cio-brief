Why cautious, not sell. The whole gap is the discount rate, not the operating forecast. At Blume-adjusted beta the same scenarios are −5.8%, and under a beta fade +7.0%. Operations are not deteriorating: FY27 guide is above $64B GMV and 14.5% GAAP operating margin [S2 p11], and 30+ day delinquencies sit in a 1.97–2.88% band.
  • Beta, briefly: how much the stock moves per 1% market move. AFRM moves about 2.5–3× the S&P 500; the analysis uses β 2.7 (between 3Y daily 2.55 and 3Y weekly 2.83), giving a 16.3% cost of equity (4.99% risk-free + 2.7 × 4.20% equity risk premium [S35][S110]).
  • At raw β the price equals the bull case ($72.6) and sits 40% above the base case ($51.9): it discounts the Investor Forum plan plus a slow fade after FY29.
  • Rolling 1Y beta has been below 2.0 on 1 of 1,202 windows (Jan 12, 2022) and has held a 2.21–2.86 band since Oct 28, 2024.
  • Robustness (analyst calc, Oct 28): the cost of equity is the most sensitive reverse-DCF input (β ±0.5 swings the base case by $21.9; terminal margin ±5pp by $21.6; terminal growth 2–4% by only $3.0). Factors explain 57% of AFRM's 1Y daily variance (43% residual).
  • Revisit triggers: rolling 1Y daily β to SPY closes below 2.0 for a full quarter (upgrade); FY27 GMV growth below the 25% Forum pace for two quarters (downgrade toward bear).

3. Business model

Affirm underwrites each purchase, earns from both merchants and consumers, and sells most loans to capital partners. It keeps about 4.2% of every dollar of volume after transaction costs.

D. Tornado: base scenario at raw β ($51.9 per share), one input flexed at a time

  • Terminal growth barely matters (a $3.0 swing for 2% → 4%) because the terminal value is 33–40% of value and sits 14 years out at a 16.3% discount rate. Terminal margin does matter: $21.6 for 17.5% → 27.5%.
  • The cost of equity is the largest input: β ±0.5 swings the base case by $21.9, and the risk premium ±0.5pp by $13.8. That is why the stance hinges on beta.
  • Even at a 30% terminal margin and 4% terminal growth the raw-β base case is $70.3, below the price; at raw β the price needs about 19–21% growth a year through FY36 even at a 30% margin (grid A).
  • TAM sanity check: FY41 GMV of $838B / $517B / $427B is 62% / 38% / 31% of today's annualized US e-commerce ($1.36T) [S119], and 3.7–7.3% of 2024 US card payments ($11.50T) [S120]. Demanding at raw β, not impossible.
  • Scenarios: bull = Card top-of-wallet, bank charter, RLTC above 4%; base = Forum pace to FY29 then fade to 12% by FY36; bear = credit and funding stress, 12% growth, 15% margin.
  • Weights matter less than beta: even equal weights across the three beta cases give $65.6 (−9.7%).
